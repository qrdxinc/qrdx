Invite rewards give you and a friend extra dynamic codes when they join QRdx through your link. They run on qrdx.dev only; a self-hosted QRdx server doesn't offer them. The [Terms of Service](/legal/terms-of-service#invite-rewards) have the full rules.

## How it works

1. Open **Settings** → **Invite friends** and copy your invite link.
2. Send it to a friend, or post it anywhere.
3. Your friend creates a QRdx account through the link, in the same visit.
4. When they make their first dynamic code within {facts.inviteQualifyDays} days of signing up, you both get {facts.inviteBonusCodes} more dynamic codes in your own workspaces.

You're rewarded for up to {facts.inviteMaxFriends} friends, so {facts.inviteMaxCodes} extra codes at most. Friends who join after that still get their {facts.inviteBonusCodes}. We email you each time a friend earns you codes, unless you've turned off notes from the QRdx team under **Settings** → **Notifications**.

## What the extra codes are

- More room for active dynamic codes in your own workspace, on any plan. On Free it raises the {facts.freeDynamicCodes}-code limit; on plans without a limit it changes nothing. Team workspaces don't get them.
- They aren't a plan or a discount, have no cash value and can't be moved to another account.
- Codes you make with them keep working like every other code, even if the extra codes are ever taken away for abuse. Only new codes then need room under your plan's limit.

**Settings** → **Invite friends** shows how many friends joined through your link, how many earned you codes and how many extra codes you have. Your workspace's **Plan & usage** page counts them in its dynamic code limit.

## What doesn't count

- A sign-up on a later visit, in another tab, or after going between the docs and the rest of the site. Nothing is stored on your friend's device, so the link only works during that visit.
- An account that already exists. An account keeps the first invite it signed up with.
- Your own other accounts, an account using your email address under another spelling, and anyone who shares a workspace with you.
- A dynamic code made more than {facts.inviteQualifyDays} days after your friend signed up. A code held for a safety check, or still waiting for its link to be checked, counts only once the check clears.

## Privacy

You see how many friends joined and earned you codes, never who they are. Your friends never see who invited them: the page they land on only says they were invited. The [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy#invite-rewards) says what's recorded and for how long.
